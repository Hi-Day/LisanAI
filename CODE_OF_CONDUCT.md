# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

LisanAI welcomes contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. We aim to make participation respectful, constructive, and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and open to constructive feedback.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish credentials, personal information, student records, or private communications without permission.
- Discuss disagreements professionally and focus on evidence and project goals.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome advances in project spaces.
- Publishing private information or credentials.
- Deliberate disruption, intimidation, or other conduct that makes participation unsafe.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through a verified private channel listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not disclose sensitive information in public issues. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, decline contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces when someone is representing the project.
