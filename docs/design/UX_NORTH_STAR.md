# LisanAI UX North Star

## Product Promise

LisanAI helps educators evaluate how students think and communicate—not merely whether they arrive at the correct answer.

The UX must therefore make the assessment process feel:

**human → guided → intelligent → transparent → actionable**

## Five Experience Principles

### 1. Start with confidence

Users should never feel lost when beginning an assessment.

### 2. Make complexity progressive

The system may be sophisticated internally, but the user should encounter only the complexity necessary for the current task.

### 3. Make AI legible

Users should know:

- when AI is working
- what AI produced
- what AI inferred
- what still needs human judgment

### 4. Protect human agency

AI recommendations should be easy to review, edit, reject, or accept.

### 5. End with an action

Assessment results should lead naturally to:

- review
- feedback
- intervention
- follow-up assessment
- instructional decision

A dashboard that merely reports numbers is incomplete.

## Role-Specific North Stars

### Teacher

> "I can create a meaningful oral assessment quickly, understand what happened, and know what to do next."

### Student

> "I understand what is expected, can answer naturally, and know where I stand."

### Admin

> "I can understand the health of the system without being overwhelmed by operational detail."

## AI Principle

> AI should reduce cognitive load, not transfer cognitive load.

If an AI feature produces more information than the user can reasonably interpret, the UX has failed even if the model output is technically correct.

## Quality Test

A strong LisanAI experience should make users think:

> "I know what is happening."

> "I know why I am seeing this."

> "I know what I can do next."

> "I remain in control."

That is the UX standard for the product.
